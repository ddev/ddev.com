[Ajith](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thampiajit) is a web developer at the Ottawa Public Library, where he works with Drupal day to day. He has been building websites with PHP for years, mostly Drupal, WordPress, and Laravel, with some React and TypeScript in the mix, and he is comfortable around Linux servers, Docker, and CI/CD. Every so often he runs into a problem on one of his personal projects that no existing tool solves the way he wants, so he builds his own and releases it as open source. That is how his two DDEV add-ons, ddev-tailscale-router and ddev-sql-sync, came about.
