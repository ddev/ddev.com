[Ajith](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thampiajit) is a web developer at the Ottawa Public Library, where he works with Drupal day to day. He has been building websites with PHP for years, mostly Drupal, WordPress, and Laravel, with some React and TypeScript in the mix, and he is comfortable around Linux servers, Docker, and CI/CD. He usually finds a workaround with the tools he already knows — Tailscale and Drush come in handy in a lot of situations. When he does build something of his own, it is because it suits his workflow better, and he releases it as open source in case anyone else finds it useful. His two DDEV add-ons, ddev-tailscale-router and ddev-sql-sync, both started that way, on his personal projects.
