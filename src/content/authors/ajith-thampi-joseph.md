[Ajith](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thampiajit) is a web developer at the Ottawa Public Library, where he works with Drupal day to day. He's been building websites with PHP for years — Drupal, WordPress, Laravel, a bit of React and TypeScript — and is happiest somewhere between an editor and a Linux terminal. He'd usually rather find a workaround with a tool he already trusts than build something new; Tailscale and Drush have rescued him more times than he can count. When a personal project needs something that doesn't exist yet, he builds it to fit his own workflow and puts it on GitHub, in case it helps someone else too. That's how his DDEV add-ons, ddev-tailscale-router and ddev-sql-sync, happened.
